About JNV Bhopal

The 12th Feb, 1993 was the most auspicious day when the foundation stone of Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya, Ratibad, Bhopal was laid down by Hon Shri Arjun Singh, the present Education Minister of Madhya Pradesh. The school campus covers around 30.67 acres of forested with its buildings connected by steep and windy paths and offering spectacular views at every turn. These buildings include the beautiful old Quad, the modern state of art infrastructure and the newly-refurbished student hostels and staff houses. The academic buildings including several classrooms are linked by a computer network. The widespread use of email and an intranet system shrinks the distances and keeps staff, students and administration in quick and easy contact with one another. The school is located on Sehore Bhopal Road at village Ratibad, Bhopal.
